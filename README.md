## 💡 Overview & Ecosystem Insights

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Newsletter Publishing**. These systems help writers, creators, journalists, and media teams publish newsletters, manage subscribers, monetize through paid subscriptions or sponsorships, and own their audience relationships.

### 📊 Sector Market Size & Dynamics
> 💡 **Market Size & Structure**: The global digital newsletter and email marketing software market is estimated at **$8.5 Billion–$10.2 Billion**, projecting growth driven by the expanding creator economy and direct-to-audience publishing models. The market is **moderately fragmented**: high-volume legacy enterprise email software (Mailchimp/Intuit) holds substantial volume, while fast-growing creator platforms (Substack, Beehiiv, Kit) compete aggressively for independent writer market share alongside robust open-source privacy-focused alternatives (Ghost, Listmonk).

### 🛠️ Additional Open-Source Components & Workflows

- **RSS-to-Newsletter Automation**: Workflows built on [n8n](https://github.com/n8n-io/n8n) or Node-RED to convert RSS/Atom feeds into automated Listmonk/Ghost broadcasts.
- **Transactional + Marketing Delivery**: Infrastructure stacks combining self-hosted Postal or Mailcow with Amazon SES for ultra-low-cost sending.
- **Template & Design Frameworks**: Open-source email layout tools such as [MJML](https://github.com/mjmlio/mjml) for responsive newsletter rendering.
