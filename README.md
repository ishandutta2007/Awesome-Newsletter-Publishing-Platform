# Awesome-Newsletter-Publishing-Platform

## Top Newsletter Publishing Platform Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Newsletter Creation, Audience Growth, Email Delivery, Paid Subscriptions, Creator Monetization & Self-Hosted Publishing*  

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Newsletter Publishing**. These systems help writers, creators, and media teams publish newsletters, manage subscribers, monetize through paid subscriptions or ads, and own (or rent) their audience relationship.



**Examples** include Beehiiv, Substack, Kit (formerly ConvertKit), Ghost, Mailchimp, MailerLite, Buttondown, Audienceful, Letterdrop, and Revue Archive (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Newsletter and mailing-list software has strong open-source options. **Listmonk** is the leading high-performance self-hosted newsletter manager, **Ghost** is a full open-source publishing platform with native newsletters and memberships, and projects like **Keila**, **Mautic**, and others provide additional alternatives. This section lists every major active project found.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[Beehiiv](https://www.beehiiv.com/)**  

  Creator-focused newsletter platform with strong growth tools (referrals/Boosts), built-in ad network, website builder, analytics, and 0% take rate on paid subscriptions.



- **[Substack](https://substack.com/)**  

  Popular writing and newsletter platform known for simplicity, built-in discovery/recommendation network, and a 10% revenue share on paid subscriptions.



- **[Kit (formerly ConvertKit)](https://kit.com/)**  

  Creator email and newsletter platform with powerful automations, segmentation, landing pages, and tools for selling digital products to an audience.



- **[Ghost (Pro)](https://ghost.org/)**  

  Hosted version of the open-source Ghost publishing platform, offering newsletters, memberships, paid subscriptions, and a modern publishing experience with 0% revenue share.



- **[Mailchimp, MailerLite](https://mailchimp.com/)**  

  Established email marketing platforms frequently used for newsletters, with broad automation, templates, and audience management features.



- **[Buttondown, Audienceful, Letterdrop](https://buttondown.email/)**  

  Indie and specialized newsletter tools emphasizing simplicity, clean writing experience, or content-to-newsletter workflows.



- **[Other newsletter & email publishing platforms](https://www.beehiiv.com/)**  

  Additional commercial solutions covering lifecycle email, creator monetization, and newsletter-as-a-service offerings.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[Listmonk](https://github.com/knadh/listmonk)**  

  Leading high-performance, self-hosted newsletter and mailing list manager. Single binary + PostgreSQL, modern dashboard, REST API, supports millions of subscribers, and excellent for technical publishers.



- **[Ghost](https://github.com/TryGhost/Ghost)**  

  Open-source publishing platform with built-in newsletters, memberships, paid subscriptions, and a beautiful editor. Can be fully self-hosted or used via Ghost(Pro). Strong emphasis on ownership and no platform revenue cut.



- **[Keila](https://github.com/pentacent/keila)**  

  Modern open-source newsletter tool built with Elixir, focused on privacy, a clean UI, and straightforward self-hosted campaign management.



- **[Mautic](https://github.com/mautic/mautic)**  

  Full open-source marketing automation platform that includes email campaigns, segmentation, and automation—suitable for more complex newsletter and lifecycle programs.



- **[phpList](https://github.com/phpList/phplist3)**  

  Long-standing open-source mailing list and newsletter manager, still used in many self-hosted environments.



- **[Sendportal and similar Laravel-based tools](https://github.com/search?q=newsletter+OR+mailing+list+self-hosted)**  

  Lighter open-source newsletter and campaign managers, often built on modern PHP frameworks.



- **[Postal and mail delivery platforms](https://github.com/postalserver/postal)**  

  Open-source mail delivery and server software that can underpin self-hosted newsletter sending infrastructure.



- **[Other self-hosted newsletter & email projects](https://github.com/search?q=listmonk+OR+newsletter+manager+OR+mailing+list+open+source)**  

  Additional community tools for subscriber management, campaign creation, and RSS-to-email automation.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **RSS-to-newsletter automation**: Scripts and n8n/Node-RED flows that turn feeds into campaigns in Listmonk or Ghost.

- **Transactional + marketing hybrids**: Open tools that combine transactional email with broadcast capabilities.

- **Subscriber import/export & GDPR helpers**: Utilities for clean list management and compliance.

- **Template & design systems**: Open email template frameworks compatible with self-hosted senders.

- **Analytics overlays**: Self-hosted analytics paired with open newsletter platforms for open-rate and engagement tracking.

- Fully owned stacks: Ghost or Listmonk + Amazon SES / Postal / other SMTP + custom domain and analytics.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**:  

The strongest open-source choices are **Listmonk** (dedicated high-performance newsletter manager) and **Ghost** (full publishing platform with native newsletters and memberships).  

**Keila** and **Mautic** offer additional paths depending on whether you want simplicity or broader marketing automation.  

These can be paired with reliable SMTP providers (or self-hosted Postal) for delivery.  

Commercial platforms (Beehiiv, Substack, Kit, Mailchimp, MailerLite, Buttondown, etc.) provide superior growth tooling, deliverability support, discovery networks, and polished creator experiences.  

Many independent publishers start on Substack or Beehiiv for growth and later move to Ghost or Listmonk for ownership and cost control at scale.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Newsletter platforms handle personal data (email addresses) and must comply with anti-spam and privacy regulations (CAN-SPAM, GDPR, CASL, etc.). Deliverability depends on proper authentication (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) and list hygiene.

- Self-hosted open-source solutions give full data ownership and no revenue share but require you to manage infrastructure, deliverability, backups, and compliance. Evaluate operational capacity carefully before self-hosting.
